<div class="agp not-content">

<p class="agp-lead">AGBoss is the production application framework for LangGraph agents in TypeScript, bringing the architectural rigor of Angular, NestJS, and Spring to multi-agent engineering. You declare typed components inside an <code>@Application</code> container; the compiler verifies every graph path, review loop, and dependency before a single model call runs.</p>

<div class="agp-features">

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">@</div>
<div>
<h3>Compile-time Path Scope & DI</h3>
<p>Agents, tools, routers, actions, arbiters, and workflows are classes wired into an <code>@Application</code> root. Dependencies arrive through constructors and contracts are replaced by subclass inheritance.</p>
<p>The chain-per-line Flow DSL tracks what each step produces (<code>.returns(Dto, &#123; about &#125;)</code>, <code>historyOf(Dto)</code>) and rejects missing or stale inputs at compile time via <code>.agboss/types.d.ts</code>.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">✓</div>
<div>
<h3>Deterministic testing & 8 unit runners</h3>
<p>Drive whole multi-instance workflows offline with <code>testWith(App)</code>, scripted models, <code>VirtualClock</code>, and <code>TestConcurrency.inOrder()</code> race control, or test any component in isolation with <code>testTool</code>, <code>testAction</code>, <code>testRouter</code>, <code>testArbiter</code>, <code>testRag</code>, <code>testJudge</code>, <code>testPii</code>, and <code>testGuardrail</code>.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">$</div>
<div>
<h3>Multi-tenant FinOps & fair queues</h3>
<p><code>@Budget</code> components cap spend per execution, per tenant, or across the application per day, week, or month (UTC). <code>@ModelProvider</code> enforces tenant-weighted queues, shared 429 cooldowns, and circuit breakers.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">⛨</div>
<div>
<h3>Boundary PII & durable execution</h3>
<p><code>@PiiPolicy</code> masks sensitive data into reversible tokens (or HMAC pseudonyms) before any prompt leaves your perimeter. Seven repository contracts with built-in <code>Sqlite*</code> WAL stores provide 30-second execution leases, CAS recovery, and two-phase <code>app.resume()</code>.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">↔</div>
<div>
<h3>Zero lock-in, inside your backend</h3>
<p>AGBoss runs inside the Node.js 22+ backend you already operate. Keep your own HTTP server, SQLite/Postgres database, OpenRouter or custom model providers, and self-hosted Langfuse tracing.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">◇</div>
<div>
<h3>Native LangGraph & AI-ready</h3>
<p>Every workflow compiles down to a standard LangGraph state graph compatible with LangGraph Studio, and ships with built-in <code>AGENTS.md</code> and <code>.claude/skills/agboss/</code> references kept in sync by <code>agboss check</code>.</p>
</div>
</div>

</div>
