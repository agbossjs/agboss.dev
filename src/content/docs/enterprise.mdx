<section class="agp-section">
<h2 class="agp-h2">What is planned for enterprise teams?</h2>
<div class="agp-features">

<div class="agp-feature" id="lts">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">LTS</div>
<div>
<h3>Long-term support</h3>
<p>Release lines with a published support window and backported fixes, so upgrades happen on your engineering schedule rather than ours.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="patches">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">0</div>
<div>
<h3>Day-0 security patches</h3>
<p>Fixes for AGBoss and its model provider integrations, shipped for supported lines as soon as a vulnerability is disclosed.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="upgrades">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">↑</div>
<div>
<h3>Automated upgrades</h3>
<p>Codemod tooling that migrates workflows, YAML profiles, and component decorators to the next major version and opens the pull request for you.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="governance">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">§</div>
<div>
<h3>Governance & compliance</h3>
<p>Immutable audit trails of every workflow run, PII policy verification reports, and FinOps cost controls your security team can sign off on.</p>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<ul>
<li><strong>Zero core lock-in:</strong> Enterprise capabilities extend the Apache-2.0 open-source core without proprietary workflow syntax changes.</li>
<li><strong>Self-hosted deployment:</strong> Run entirely inside your own VPC, Kubernetes cluster, or on-premise Node.js infrastructure.</li>
<li><strong>Direct engineering escalation:</strong> Priority architectural review and incident support from the core framework maintainers.</li>
</ul>
