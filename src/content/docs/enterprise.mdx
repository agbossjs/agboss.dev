<div class="agp-features">

<div>

<p>Release lines with a published support window, so upgrades happen on your schedule rather than ours.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="patches">
<div class="agp-feature-icon">0</div>

</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="upgrades">

</div>
</div>

<div class="agp-feature" id="governance">
