title: About AGBoss
description: What AGBoss is and why it exists.
---

AGBoss is an application framework layered on top of [LangGraph](https://www.langchain.com/langgraph) — complementing it rather than competing with it head-on. The framework is licensed under Apache 2.0 and embeds into an existing Node.js backend: bring your own backend, no ecosystem lock-in.
